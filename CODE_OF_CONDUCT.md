# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Discuss code, behavior, evidence,
and trade-offs without attacking people. Welcome contributors of different
backgrounds and experience levels, and make technical decisions accessible to
the people affected by them.

Examples of welcome behavior include:

- giving actionable review feedback;
- acknowledging uncertainty and unverified claims;
- respecting privacy, accessibility, and consent;
- accepting correction without retaliation; and
- keeping humor aimed at the code or situation, never a person.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, doxxing, deliberate
misrepresentation, and publishing another person's private information are not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns through a private repository-owner channel. Do not post
private evidence in a public issue. Maintainers may remove content, restrict
participation, or take other proportionate action after reviewing the facts.

This code applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, review
threads, and other project spaces.
