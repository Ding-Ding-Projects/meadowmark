Nothing in Meadowmark is ever for sale — no store, no premium tier, no ads, no nagging. Premium currency is earned only. Meadowmark 入面冇嘢係賣㗎——冇商店、冇付費會籍、冇廣告、唔會煩你俾錢。所有貨幣都係打機賺返嚟。
