Documentation / Train

🚂 Train 火車

What it does 做乜嘢

Three outbound wagons wanting goods, returning building materials — planks, bricks, nails, glass, slabs, paint, screws — on a twenty-to-sixty-minute round trip. 三個出發車卡要貨，用 20 到 60 分鐘一個來回，換返建築材料——木板、磚頭、釘、玻璃、石板、油漆、螺絲。

How it works 點運作

Materials are the only route to town buildings and barn upgrades, which is the deliberate mechanism that stops coins alone from buying the whole city — a player with plenty of coins but no train activity still cannot expand freely. 材料係起小鎮建築同升級倉庫嘅唯一途徑，呢個係刻意嘅機制，令淨係有金幣都買唔晒成個城市——就算個玩家有好多金幣但冇乘搭過火車，都唔可以隨便擴建。

Failure modes 失敗情況

A wagon that cannot be filled (because the requested good is not yet produced) simply waits — it does not block the other two wagons, and it does not expire. 如果一個車卡填唔到（要求嘅貨仲未整到），佢會就咁等——唔會阻住其他兩個車卡，亦都唔會過期。

What is not yet implemented 仲未整好嘅部分

No public build of Meadowmark has shipped yet (see the changelog). The material-drop table per wagon trip, and its coupling to the town-building material costs, is balance data checked for reachability before it ships. Meadowmark 暫時未有公開發佈（睇更新日誌）。每次車程嘅材料掉落表，同佢同小鎮建築材料成本嘅關係，都係平衡數據，出街之前會做可達性檢查。
