Documentation / Boosters

⚡ Boosters 加速道具

What it does 做乜嘢

Boosters are earned only — never purchasable — and double grow speed, double factory speed, speed the train, refill energy, reroll orders, or grant temporary barn overflow. 加速道具淨係靠賺——永遠買唔到——效果包括生長速度加倍、工廠速度加倍、加快火車、回滿能量、重擲訂單，或者暫時加大倉容量。

How it works 點運作

Boosters are the game's only route to a temporary systemic speed-up, and because they are earned only, no amount of coins or cash can substitute for playing to get one — this is one of the concrete mechanisms behind the project's 'nothing is ever for sale' commitment. 加速道具係遊戲入面唯一可以攞到暫時系統性加速嘅途徑，而因為佢淨係靠賺，唔理你有幾多金幣定 Cash 都換唔到——呢個係「Meadowmark 入面冇任何嘢係賣」呢個承諾背後其中一個具體機制。

Failure modes 失敗情況

A booster's duration or effect should be visibly countable down on screen while active, so a player is never surprised by it expiring — this is a general non-blocking-notification and status-visibility requirement, not specific to boosters, but boosters are the clearest case where it matters. 加速道具生效期間，佢嘅剩餘時間或者效果應該喺畫面度清楚可以睇到倒數，令玩家唔會俾佢突然完咗嚇親——呢個係一個通用嘅非阻擋式通知同狀態可見性要求，唔係淨係加速道具先有，但加速道具係最明顯要做到呢點嘅例子。

What is not yet implemented 仲未整好嘅部分

No public build of Meadowmark has shipped yet (see the changelog). The full booster list, their sources (achievements, chests, dailies), and their exact multipliers are balance data. Meadowmark 暫時未有公開發佈（睇更新日誌）。完整加速道具清單、佢哋嘅來源（成就、寶箱、每日任務），同確實嘅倍率，都係平衡數據。
