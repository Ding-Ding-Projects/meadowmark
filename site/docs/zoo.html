Documentation / Zoo

🦁 Zoo 動物園

What it does 做乜嘢

Unlocking from level 25. Enclosures by habitat: grass, water, rock and arctic. Animal cards drop from ship chests, the mine and orders, and collecting a full card set for a species hatches it. 25 級解鎖。圍欄按棲息地分：草地、水、岩石、極地。動物卡由船寶箱、礦場同訂單掉落，儲齊一種物種嘅全套卡就會孵化出嚟。

How it works 點運作

Animals draw visitors, visitors pay coins and zoo currency, and matching a species to its habitat or grouping families pays bonuses — so enclosure layout is a real decision with a real payoff, rather than a shelf to place collected animals on. 動物會吸引遊客，遊客俾金幣同動物園貨幣，物種夾啱棲息地或者將同一家族擺埋一齊都有獎勵——所以圍欄擺位係一個真係有回報嘅決定，唔係淨係擺低收集到嘅動物嘅架子。

Failure modes 失敗情況

A species missing cards from its set simply cannot hatch yet — there is no partial-hatch state, and the interface should say exactly which cards are still needed rather than a vague 'incomplete'. 缺卡嘅物種就係未孵化到——冇部分孵化呢種狀態，介面應該清楚講仲差邊幾張卡，而唔係含糊咁講「未完成」。

What is not yet implemented 仲未整好嘅部分

No public build of Meadowmark has shipped yet (see the changelog). The species list, habitat bonuses and card-drop tables are balance data; the enclosure layout scoring (habitat match, family grouping) is part of the same validated dataset. Meadowmark 暫時未有公開發佈（睇更新日誌）。物種清單、棲息地獎勵同抽卡掉落表都係平衡數據；圍欄擺位計分（棲息地夾唔夾、家族有冇擺埋一齊）都係同一套已驗證數據嘅一部分。
