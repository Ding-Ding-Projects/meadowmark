🚁 Helicopter 直升機

What it does 做乜嘢

Two small fast orders every half hour, paying coins and reputation. A filled reputation bar opens a chest of cash, boosters and animal cards. 每半個鐘兩張快速細訂單，俾金幣同信譽。信譽條儲滿會開一個有 Cash、加速道具同動物卡嘅寶箱。

How it works 點運作

The helicopter is designed as a fast, low-friction consumer alongside the order board's slower six-slot rotation — a player checking in briefly can still make meaningful progress through helicopter orders alone. 直升機設計成一個快、低門檻嘅消耗途徑，同訂單板嗰種較慢嘅六位輪轉並存——玩家就算得閒得少少，都可以淨係靠直升機訂單有實質進度。

Failure modes 失敗情況

As with orders, helicopter requests are drawn from producible goods, so a request that cannot be fulfilled at all should never appear. 同訂單一樣，直升機要求嘅都係整得到嘅貨，所以理論上唔應該出現完全整唔到嘅要求。

What is not yet implemented 仲未整好嘅部分

No public build of Meadowmark has shipped yet (see the changelog). The reputation-bar-to-chest reward table (cash amount, booster odds, animal card odds) is balance data alongside the rest of the economy. Meadowmark 暫時未有公開發佈（睇更新日誌）。信譽條開寶箱嘅獎勵表（Cash 數量、加速道具機率、動物卡機率）同其他經濟數據一齊管理。
