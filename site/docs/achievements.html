🏆 Achievements 成就

What it does 做乜嘢

Around forty tiered achievements pay cash and coins, and are one of the three earned routes to expansion permits alongside the mine and ship chests. 大約四十個分級成就，俾 Cash 同金幣，亦係擴地許可證嘅三大賺取途徑之一，同礦場同船寶箱並列。

How it works 點運作

Being tiered means an achievement typically has several thresholds (do X five times, then twenty-five times, then a hundred times) rather than a single one-off unlock, giving long-term goals across the whole span of a save rather than a burst that is exhausted early. 分級即係話一個成就通常有幾個門檻（做五次、廿五次、一百次），而唔係一次性解鎖，等玩家喺成個存檔嘅生命週期都有長遠目標，而唔係一開始就爆晒。

Failure modes 失敗情況

An achievement's current progress toward its next tier should always be visible on demand, so a player is never left guessing how close they are. 成就去到下一級嘅目前進度，應該隨時睇得到，唔會令玩家要靠估自己差幾遠。

What is not yet implemented 仲未整好嘅部分

The published v0.1.0-22 desktop baseline is available (see the changelog). The achievement list, its tiers and its exact payouts are balance data, verified against the reward pipeline the same way as boosters and dailies. 已發佈嘅 v0.1.0-22 桌面基準而家可以下載（睇更新日誌）。成就清單、分級同確實回報都係平衡數據，同加速道具、每日任務一樣，用同一套獎勵流程驗證。
