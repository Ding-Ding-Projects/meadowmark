Meadowmark Meadowmark

A real 3D town-and-farm building game for Windows, in the spirit of Township. Grow crops, run factories, fill orders, and grow a living town — rendered in three.js, on the wall clock, entirely on your machine. 一個真正 3D 嘅小鎮同農場建設遊戲，靈感嚟自 Township，喺 Windows 度玩。種田、開工廠、交訂單、起小鎮——用 three.js 畫，跟真實時鐘走，全部喺你部機度運行。

Nothing in Meadowmark is ever for sale. Meadowmark 入面冇任何嘢係賣㗎。

No store, no premium tier, no advertising, no prompt asking anyone for money — ever. Premium currency ("Cash") is earned only, through play. Every capability is available to everyone who runs it. 冇商店、冇付費會籍、冇廣告，永遠唔會叫你俾錢。「Cash」呢種進階貨幣淨係靠打機賺——冇得買。裝咗個遊戲嘅人，人人都用得晒所有功能。

Every system in the game 遊戲入面每一個系統

This overview matches the project's design plan exactly. See the documentation for a full article on each one. 呢個概覽同項目嘅設計計劃完全一致。每個系統嘅詳細文章，睇文件。

🌾 Fields & crops 田地同作物

Six starting plots, buyable up to sixty. Seventeen crops from a two-minute wheat harvest to a twenty-hour vanilla harvest, plant-all and harvest-all with honest partial results.初始六塊田，最多買到六十塊。十七種作物，由兩分鐘嘅小麥到二十個鐘嘅雲呢拿，全種同全收都會誠實話你知有幾多做得成。

🐔 Animals 動物

Six species — chickens, cows, sheep, pigs, goats, bees — fed by animal feed, each with an upgradeable shed.六種動物——雞、牛、羊、豬、山羊、蜜蜂——用飼料餵，每種都有可以升級嘅棚。

🏭 Factories & production chains 工廠同生產鏈

About twenty-two factories, chains up to three tiers deep, production continues while the app is closed, and a full barn pauses a queue rather than discarding output.大約二十二間工廠，生產鏈最多三層深，關咗個 app 都繼續生產，倉滿咗就暫停唔會扔嘢。

📋 Order board 訂單板

Six slots, only ever asking for goods you can actually produce, paying coins, experience and reputation stars.六個訂單位，淨係會要你整得到嘅貨，俾金幣、經驗同信譽星。

🚂 Train 火車

Three wagons trading goods for building materials — the only route to town buildings and barn upgrades.三個車卡，用貨換建築材料——起小鎮建築同升級倉庫嘅唯一途徑。

🚁 Helicopter 直升機

Two small fast orders every half hour; a filled reputation bar opens a chest.每半個鐘兩張快速細訂單；信譽條滿咗開寶箱。

🚢 Ship 船

From level 18. Six crates on a twenty-four-hour window, clearing all six opens a chest with expansion permits.18 級開放。六個木箱，24 小時一輪，六個都清完開個有擴地許可嘅寶箱。

🏘️ The town 小鎮

Houses, thirteen community buildings gated by population, decorations that pay town charm, roads that villagers actually walk.屋、十三種按人口解鎖嘅社區建築、加人氣值嘅裝飾、村民真係會行嘅馬路。

🗺️ Expansions 擴地

Around forty land parcels, costing coins and permits earned from ship chests, the mine and achievements.大約四十塊地，要金幣同用船寶箱、礦場同成就賺返嚟嘅許可證。

🦁 Zoo 動物園

From level 25. Four habitats, animal cards, and a real layout decision — matching species to habitat pays bonuses.25 級開放。四種棲息地、動物卡，擺位真係要諗——物種同棲息地夾啱有獎勵。

⛏️ Mine 礦場

From level 22. A grid of rock hiding ore, gems, tools and artifact fragments, regenerating daily.22 級開放。石格入面藏住礦石、寶石、工具同文物碎片，每日重生。

⚡ Boosters, achievements & dailies 加速道具、成就同每日任務

Earned-only boosters, around forty tiered achievements, and five daily tasks seeded from the local date.淨係靠賺嘅加速道具、大約四十個分級成就，同五個跟本機日期出嘅每日任務。

🏡 The village — entirely local 村莊——完全本機

Named offline villagers, a weekly local regatta. The game makes no network calls at all — no accounts, no telemetry, no other players.有名有姓嘅離線村民、每週本機龍舟賽。呢個遊戲完全唔會上網——冇帳號、冇遙測、冇其他玩家。

When a release does ship, it will be an unsigned Squirrel.Windows installer. Windows will show an "unknown publisher" / SmartScreen warning — that is expected, not a bug, because this project permanently does not use code signing. 將來出版本嘅時候，會係一個未簽署嘅 Squirrel.Windows 安裝程式。Windows 會出「未知發行者」/ SmartScreen 警告——呢個係預期會出現，唔係 bug，因為呢個項目永遠都唔會用程式碼簽署。

Platform平台Requirement需求

Network網絡None required. The game makes no network calls of any kind, ever.完全唔使上網。呢個遊戲永遠都唔會發任何網絡請求。
Cost費用Free, forever, with no purchases of any kind.永遠免費，完全冇任何形式嘅付費。
