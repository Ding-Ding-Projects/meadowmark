Voice

A farm-and-town game that never asks for money and never makes a network call. The brand voice is warm and plain-spoken, not corporate: "grow crops, run factories, fill orders, grow a living town" rather than "engage in agricultural simulation." The one thing repeated everywhere — footer, hero, download panel — is the honest claim that nothing is ever for sale.

Imagery mood

Golden-hour farm gradients — the same three container colours the hero section blends live. Warm, inviting, never neon; this is a place to farm, not a SaaS dashboard.
