Why this exists

The live site's --mm-bg and --mm-surface were identical, so a card never separated from the page behind it. This is a real M3 tonal system: five 0–100 tonal ramps (harvest green primary, harvest gold secondary, sky blue tertiary, plus neutral and neutral-variant for surfaces), with the standard M3 role assignments pulled from them — never hand-picked to look plausible. See design/tools/generate-tonal-palettes.py and design/tools/build-tokens.py for exactly how each hex value below was derived, and that script's collision report for proof no two distinct roles accidentally share a value.
